About

Each tool pulls free model data from Open-Meteo straight into your browser. Nothing is stored on a server. Your location, weights and theme are remembered on this device only.

Tool 01

MODEL BLEND

Up to 11 global and regional models side by side, blended with weights you set. Shows spread bands, accumulated precip and snow, and a daily table with a confidence rating for each day.

OPEN → Tool 02

THUNDER

An hour-by-hour thunder signal on the same NORMAL / MONITOR / PREPARE / ACT scale as Heads-Up. It comes from model thunder codes and CAPE, with lifted index, CIN and deep-layer shear for context.

OPEN → Tool 03

SEVERE

The severe environment from each model's sounding: 0–1 and 0–6 km shear, 0–1 and 0–3 km SRH, Bunkers motion, lapse rates, LCL, freezing level, and fixed-layer STP and SCP. Includes an hour-by-hour tier strip, hail, wind and tornado ingredient flags, and a hodograph.

OPEN → Tool 04

WINTER

Snow odds from about 140 ensemble members (GEFS, ECMWF, ICON, GEM). Shows the chance of reaching each total, an accumulation plume, the temperature spread, and a day-by-day breakdown.
