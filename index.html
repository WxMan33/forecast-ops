Rows marked NWS are NWS's own day (≈6a–6p) and night (≈6p–6a) periods. NWS is also one weighted source inside the blend values. Every other value uses the same sources, weights and calculation as its tool page: temperatures, precip and gusts from MODEL BLEND (models, ECMWF ENS, GEFS and NWS, using the blend recipe you picked there), snow chances from WINTER, the thunder signal from THUNDER. Change a model's weight on a tool page and this summary follows it. Overview cards use the printed local planning periods, with night lows and daytime highs explicitly labeled. At 2 PM, tonight and tomorrow lead; a smaller rest-of-today summary remains visible until 5 PM. Partial current-hour precipitation is counted in full and may include the elapsed part of that hour. The daily Blend table retains calendar days, so it can differ from these period values.

What the colors mean
These are the fixed Heads-Up status colors. In these forecast tools they show signal strength in model guidance, from LOW to HIGH. They don't tell anyone to act. Detected lightning and clearance decisions stay in Heads-Up.

About

Each page pulls free model data from Open-Meteo straight into your browser. Nothing is stored on a server. Your location, saved places, weights and "last check" snapshots stay on this device. Use the printer button for a clean one-page briefing.
