# Forecast guidance support

Event Manager shows support for the remaining planning window, from the current hour (or setup, if later) through teardown. Model Blend follows its hourly forecast controls (24, 48, 72 hours or custom). Times use the forecast point's zone. These diagnostics do not change weights, forecasts, hazard ratings or verification records.

Coverage and agreement are distinct. Good coverage means every selected, applicable source supplies every requested hour of temperature, rain amount, hourly rain probability and gusts, with at least two source systems applicable to each field. An unavailable source, short forecast, variable gap or incomplete ensemble makes coverage Limited. No supplied hours means Unavailable. Endpoint fields never requested (for example ensemble gusts) and explicit recipe exclusions do not count as missing. Complete means every overlapping whole hour, including the hours on either side of a half-hour phase boundary. It does not mean accuracy is verified.

Agreement compares only source systems that cover every hour and have positive recipe weight throughout the window. Each ensemble counts once, with the mean of members complete over that whole window. This is a comparison of systems, not the full range of ensemble members, a calibrated probability or independent-model confidence. Temperature, hourly rain probability and gusts show the largest same-hour range across those systems. With one comparable system, agreement is unavailable.

Rain timing compares the first hour in the displayed window reaching 0.01 inch. This existing hourly rain-amount screening level is a diagnostic, not a new threat threshold. First wet hour is not necessarily storm onset, particularly if rain has already started. Sources that stay below that amount are counted explicitly. Small amounts and nonzero rain probabilities remain possible when no source reaches it. NWS interval totals remain in coverage using the engine's existing interval aggregation, but are excluded from hourly onset comparisons because prorating an interval cannot establish an onset hour.

Freshness retains original browser retrieval times and labels cached guidance older than one minute as a saved copy. Missing retrieval times are unknown. Provider-reported latest-run metadata is not a confirmed run for the forecast response. Metadata older than twice its stated update interval is labelled as an older-metadata cue, not a confirmed provider outage or delayed forecast. Confirmed native GRIB cycle times are identified separately. Ages refresh every minute and on returning to Event Manager, and with the normal one-minute display refresh on Model Blend; this makes no extra weather-data requests. Printing refreshes the diagnostics and adds a compact support line to the existing two-page briefing.

Briefing history does not claim improvement or worsening when future-hour coverage, variable contributors or missingness changes, even when the overall source list is unchanged. Same-source rain timing shifts of at least two hours are described as timing changes, never an all-clear. New official alerts remain visible. Diagnostics and comparison text are not sent to analytics or added to the OPE handoff.

Validation: `node tests/forecast-support.test.js`, `node tests/event-briefing-history.test.js`, the offline event briefing browser/print suite and simulated source-failure suite. Browser checks run in the PR workflow.
