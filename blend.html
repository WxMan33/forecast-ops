How to read this table

Weighted mean of the blend sources: models, ECMWF ENS, GEFS and NWS. Tap, focus or hover a column heading for its definition. Click a day to zoom the chart to it and see its source breakdown. Temp agreement is how close the sources' daily highs are (ensembles as their members' mean high), with their actual range; it says nothing about rain or timing. When the table is narrow, columns that don't fit move into each day's details (the ▸ button), nothing is dropped.
