# CVG precipitation verification

The Precipitation tab of verify.html reads reports/precip.json from the private data repository. Reports are generated during existing scoring jobs; no new forecasts are collected and no blend weights change.

Amount and probability comparisons use separate exact common-case joins: primary scheduled capture, source, climate target, valid date, capture edition, target convention and full UTC window. All archive forecasts in a case must come from one normalization version (mixes are excluded and counted); a personal forecast's starting-snapshot version is provenance only, and a personal case whose target definition has changed is excluded. Choose noon or evening, lead day 1–7, date range and sources. All selected sources must have the same observation on a case. Duplicate or missing source cases are excluded. One source can be inspected alone, clearly labelled. Unique dates and seven-day blocks accompany pair counts; repeated forecasts are not independent weather events.

The CVG climate day is midnight–midnight EST throughout the year (1 a.m.–1 a.m. EDT in summer). Amounts are inches of liquid-equivalent precipitation, including melted snow. Trace is below the 0.01-inch measurable threshold, not an invented measurable amount.

Amount MAE and signed bias use forecast minus observation. Wet/dry outcomes classify forecast and observed amounts at >=0.01 inch. ACIS amounts flagged as accumulated over several days are never used as a single day's amount or wet/dry outcome. Hit rate is hits/(hits+misses); false-alarm ratio is false alarms/(hits+false alarms); wet-frequency bias is forecast-wet count/observed-wet count. Undefined ratios remain blank. NWS timing-prorated amounts are excluded by default; an explicit toggle includes them with flags and archived timing bounds retained.

Probability verification accepts only archived ensemble frequencies for >=0.01 inch over that same full climate day. The archive must identify a positive member count; blend probabilities must also identify their ensemble-only method. NWS published period PoP, max hourly PoP, unspecified TV probabilities and probability inferred from an amount do not enter this daily comparison. The blend's probability describes its ensemble contributors, even when NWS contributes heavily to its quantitative amount forecast.

Brier score is mean squared probability error. Reliability bins are [0,0.1), ..., [0.9,1], with counts, mean forecast probability and observed wet fraction. Empty bins remain empty. Diagram points have the same accessible tabular values. These are descriptive finite-sample results, not calibrated probabilities or skill rankings. At least 30 unique dates and varied weather are needed before conclusions; any future blend selection must be tested on unseen dates.

My Forecast's primary liquid-precipitation amounts participate when common cases exist. Personal forecasts do not yet accept probability entries. TV collection, forecast normalization, schedules, raw evidence and frozen recipes are unchanged. JSON export retains provenance and observation quality/revision flags.
