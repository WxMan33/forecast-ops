How signals are set
HIGH Half or more of the model weight shows thunder, with CAPE ≥ 250 J/kg.
ELEVATED A quarter or more of the weight shows thunder, or CAPE ≥ 1000 J/kg with precip chance ≥ 40%.
MARGINAL Any model shows thunder, or CAPE ≥ 250 J/kg with precip chance ≥ 20%.
LTD Limited data. Shown on top of any signal level when thunder codes, CAPE or precip chance are missing for that hour, so the signal could be understated. Hover for what's missing.
LOW None of the above.
These are the Heads-Up status colors, used here for signal strength in model guidance. They are not an instruction to act, a lightning forecast, an SPC outlook or a warning. Detected lightning and clearance decisions belong to Heads-Up.
