How tiers are set
ACT Half or more of the model weight shows thunder, with CAPE ≥ 250 J/kg.
PREPARE A quarter or more of the weight shows thunder, or CAPE ≥ 1000 J/kg with precip chance ≥ 40%.
MONITOR Any model shows thunder, or CAPE ≥ 250 J/kg with precip chance ≥ 20%.
NORMAL None of the above.
A simple, transparent rule on model output, meant for planning. It is not a lightning forecast, an SPC outlook or a warning.
