How signals are set
HIGH 50%+ chance of 4" or more.
ELEVATED 40%+ chance of 1", or 20%+ chance of 4".
MARGINAL 10%+ chance of 1", or 30%+ chance of any measurable snow.
LOW None of the above.
Chances are shares of ensemble members, weighted by ensemble. They describe the model guidance, not a calibrated probability.
About these numbers
Snowfall is each member's own model snow, which Open-Meteo converts from water at about 7:1. Cold, fluffy events can run higher and warm, wet ones lower, and it doesn't account for melting on warm ground.
A member only counts toward a total if it has at least 90% of the hours in that window. Members with gaps are left out rather than counted as zero, and the plume line stops where a member's data stops.
