SCP

How these are computed. Open-Meteo gives each model's CAPE, CIN and lifted index, plus winds, temperatures and heights on pressure levels. Shear, helicity, Bunkers motion, lapse rates and LCL are calculated here from those levels, about 50–150 hPa apart, and the 10 m wind. STP and SCP are the fixed-layer versions: STP uses 0–1 km SRH and 0–6 km shear, and SCP uses 0–3 km SRH and 0–6 km shear in place of the effective-layer values. Expect a rougher picture than SPC mesoanalysis or a full-resolution sounding. Which kind of CAPE each model reports varies.
